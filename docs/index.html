New in 5.3: smarter background removal, and a recorder made for tutorials

Ctrl+Alt+E opens on whatever image is on the clipboard. The tools, the keys, the menus and the right-click menus follow Photoshop, so what you already know works here — and a screenshot goes from paste to annotated PNG without leaving the tray. Version 5.3 cuts backgrounds out far more cleanly, and its screen recorder follows the pointer, zooms, records your voice, highlights clicks and can log every action. The editor keeps several documents open in tabs, starts new ones from Photoshop-style presets (A4, A3, 16:9, 21:9 and dozens more), removes a photo's background with an AI model that runs on your PC, and prints with a preview.

  • Alt+Ctrl+B Remove Background, offline.One click makes everything but the subject transparent, like remove.bg. It uses the IS-Net AI model, which ships with MicroApp and runs on your PC in a second or two. Nothing is uploaded. With a selection, only the part inside it changes.
  • Ctrl+N New documents from presets, in tabs.Print sizes A0–A6, Letter and Legal; ratios 16:9, 21:9, 4:3, 1:1 and 9:16; photo, web, mobile and video sizes; or any size in px, in, cm or mm at any resolution. Each document opens in its own tab, and Ctrl+Tab switches between them.
  • Ctrl+P Print with a preview.The page drawn to scale with the picture on it: drag it into place, centre it, scale it to fit or give it an exact size, and see the resolution it will print at before anything is sent.
  • M Selections that behave.Marquee, lasso, magic wand, with add, subtract and intersect. Transform Selection reshapes the marching ants and leaves the pixels alone; Free Transform moves them.
  • Ctrl+T Scale, rotate, skew, distort, perspective.Photoshop's modifiers, numeric entry in the options bar, Enter to apply.
  • B Brush, eraser, clone stamp, gradient, dodge and burn.Size, hardness, opacity and flow, with the bracket keys where you expect them. Click once and Shift-click somewhere else for a straight line; Shift-drag locks a stroke to an axis.
  • Ctrl+L Levels, Curves, Hue/Saturation, Color Balance.Every adjustment previews live, and the filters run over the selection only.
  • Ctrl+J Layers with blend modes and styles.Lift a selection onto its own layer, then give it a drop shadow, glow, stroke or colour overlay. Fifty steps of history, and an asset library for the logos you stamp on everything.

The rest of the tray menu

Every feature has a hot key you can change, and one window holds the lot — change a key there and the feature's own settings window agrees. Nothing runs in the background except the tray icon itself; there is no account, no telemetry and no server.

Ctrl+Alt+V

Paste as keystrokes

Three typing engines, because no single one works everywhere. The default sends scan codes, which is what gets text into VM consoles and locked-down fields. Set the speed, a start delay, and whether Enter is pressed at the end.

Ctrl+Shift+O

Read text off the screen

Drag over a browser, a PDF, a video frame or a screenshot and the text comes back through Windows' own OCR — no upload. Ctrl+Alt+T does it without OCR: it picks the exact text out of any control, like a colour picker for words.

Ctrl+Alt+S

Capture a region

The screen freezes, you drag a frame, and the handles let you fix it before you take it. Lock the ratio or the exact pixel size when you need every capture to match.

Ctrl+Alt+G

Record a GIF or an MP4

The same frame records: an animated GIF for a bug report, or Ctrl+Alt+R an H.264 video with system sound, your microphone or both mixed, no time limit. While it records, the camera can follow the pointer and zoom from 50 % to 400 %; clicks glow, Ctrl+Alt+P pauses, and an optional action log lists every click and key press by the second.

Ctrl+Shift+N

Notes that save themselves

A scratch-pad opens where the cursor is and keeps every word as you type, with spell check, an archive, and Bangla phonetic typing — write ami, get আমি. Optionally the same notes on every PC, mirrored through a free database you own.
